Soldier model: "Soldier Full Tactical Gear" by DanlyVostok
https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/soldier-full-tactical-gear-850593a8c7114c188395ba1849a66eb9
Licensed under CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Changes: stray sphere and eyes removed, meshes joined and decimated (82k -> 16k
triangles), textures resized to 512 px, grey (friendly) and subdued olive
(enemy) colour variants of the clothing and kit textures added.

Motion capture: "Premade Mocap Pack" by Haley Tuffles (free for personal and
commercial use).
Walk / run gait: Rokoko, free "16 walk and run cycle animations" pack
(https://www.rokoko.com/resources/rokoko-mocap-16-free-walk-and-run-cycle-animations),
free for any project including commercial; only averaged foot / hip paths
derived from three clips are stored (scripts/gait_mocap.gd).
