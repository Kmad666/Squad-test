Squad Modeller

Build PSX-style characters, weapons and buildings from parts, then export a .glb for Squad Test.

Building

  • Drag a part from the left onto the model. Body parts, clothes and armour snap to their place on the skeleton (drop a boot anywhere on a leg); pouches, spikes and bits stick where you drop them. Click a part in the list to add it without dragging.
  • Symmetry gives every part a mirrored twin on the other side. Edit either one and the twin follows. Unlink splits them.
  • Click a part to select it, then drag it to slide it across the surface. Use Move / Rotate / Scale for exact tweaks.
  • Every part has colours (main, pattern ink, trim, detail), a pattern, size and shape sliders (taper, bulge, bend, lean, twist, sides).
  • With nothing selected, the right panel's Colours used recolours every part using a colour at once.

Keys

W E R move / rotate / scale · Del delete · Ctrl+D duplicate · Ctrl+Z / Y undo / redo · M symmetry · G snap · P PSX · Q turn 90° · F frame · Esc deselect · Ctrl+S save

Mouse: left drag orbits, right drag pans, wheel zooms.

Into the game

  • Characters are built on Squad Test's own soldier skeleton, so every part already follows the game's walk, crouch and aim animations. Rifle ready and Walk preview them.
  • Export .glb writes one mesh per bone (hips, torso, head, upper_arm_L ... foot_R), weapons as rifle plus a muzzle point, buildings as one mesh. The design is stored inside the .glb too, so you can open it here again.
  • Your work autosaves in this browser for each mode. Use Save to keep a file.
